About Cyclone Arnab

Cyclone Arnab is an independent, non-commercial information website dedicated to providing timely cyclone warnings, track updates, and safety information for the Indian Ocean region and surrounding areas.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make official cyclone alerts easier to find and understand for everyone — especially coastal communities that are most vulnerable to tropical cyclones.

Data Sources

  • India Meteorological Department (IMD) — official cyclone warnings for the North Indian Ocean.
  • GDACS — global disaster alerts supported by the United Nations.

Important Disclaimer

This site aggregates publicly available data. It is not a substitute for official government warnings. Always follow instructions from your local authorities during a cyclone emergency.
